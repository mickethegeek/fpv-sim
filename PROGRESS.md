## Direction for the hover-throttle decision (Claude, from the main chat, 2026-11-16 ~10:52 UTC; Mike asked "is 30% hover accurate?")
- Real motors sit between linear and square: thrust tables for 2207-class motors typically give roughly a third of max thrust at half throttle, i.e. thrust ≈ throttle^1.4–1.6, not throttle². **Source it** (published thrust tables for a 2207 on 6S), then fit the exponent. With thrust-to-weight 8–9 that lands hover near **20–25 %**, matching guides (15–20 % for powerful rigs).
- Then set the PlayStation throttle mid to that hover point.
- Add a **"Trainer"** preset next to "5-inch freestyle": thrust-to-weight about 4–5, gentler rates, hover about 40 %. Beginners start there; switching presets is one click. Cite where beginner quads sit.
- Mike can override any of this.

- **For M10d (yaw climb), what the M10b leg found (2026-11-17 ~10:45 Zurich):**
  - Cause in the sim: `fcUpdate` (src/flightcontroller.js) mixes in **thrust** space (sum of thrust kept), then the airmode-style shift raises the collective when a motor would go below idle. At hover (~13 % of max thrust per motor) a big yaw torque drives two motors to idle, so the shift lifts all four: that is the climb.
  - Betaflight (https://betaflight.com/docs/wiki/guides/current/Mixer-Types): default `mixer_type = LEGACY`; "LEGACY tries to keep requested throttle position as long as possible, till the point where it cannot maintain current throttle position it will drastically start to change throttle to still get desired authority." So a real quad does change throttle once the mix saturates; how much is not stated. Real Betaflight mixes in motor-command space (not thrust space): check that and the yaw limits (`pidsum_limit_yaw`) in `src/main/flight/mixer.c` / `pid.c`; raw.githubusercontent.com was **not reachable** from this leg (permission request not answered), try again or find a docs page quoting them.
  - Pilot report (https://intofpv.com/t-drone-throttling-upwards-aggressively-when-yawing, 5-inch, BF 4.4/4.5): climbs on "really sharp yaw turns", after a firmware update "barely noticeable in the goggles"; a log reader (mstc): "the motors are maxed out and spun down all the way to idle in order to produce the needed yaw". No numbers. Suggests a real but small effect on sharp yaw; the sim's 5.2 m in 2 s at full yaw looks too big.
